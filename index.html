Participation And Data Coverage

Shows where students attempted a case, completed the simulation, submitted an evaluation, and had performance data available for score summaries.

Definitions: Attempted means the cleaned data include a selected attempt for that case. Completed simulation means the selected attempt reached the End Chat marker. Evaluation submitted means the platform has a completed evaluation record. Performance data available means that case can be included in score summaries.
